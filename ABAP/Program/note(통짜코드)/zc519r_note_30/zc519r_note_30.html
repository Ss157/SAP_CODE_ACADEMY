* loop를 사용해서 lt_scarr의 모든 레코드를 lt_spfli에 추가하기
* carrid가 JL이면 KA로 바꿔서 넣고 cityfrom에 Incheon을 쓰기
LOOP AT lt_scarr INTO ls_scarr.

  MOVE-CORRESPONDING ls_scarr TO ls_spfli.

  CASE ls_spfli-carrid.
    WHEN 'JL'.
      ls_spfli-carrid   = 'KA'.
      ls_spfli-cityfrom = 'Incheon'.
  ENDCASE.

  APPEND ls_spfli TO lt_spfli.
  CLEAR : ls_spfli.

ENDLOOP.

MOVE-CORRESPONDING lt_spfli TO lt_sflight KEEPING TARGET LINES.

cl_demo_output=>display( lt_sflight ).
